Ghost Score Explained: How to Spot Instagram Bots & Dead Accounts

A Ghost Score is a 0–100 rating that estimates how likely a profile is a bot, an empty account, or long-inactive — so you can clean your community without guessing.

Why “ghost followers” matter

Many Instagram accounts accumulate followers who never engage: purchased bots, abandoned profiles, or spam accounts. They inflate your follower count but hurt reach and make “who doesn’t follow me back” lists noisy. Tools that only list non-followers miss this quality problem. Ghost Score adds a second layer: who is real enough to keep?

How Ghost Score works (0–100)

In Instagram Unfollowers PRO, Ghost Score combines simple, transparent signals available from the public profile data Instagram already shows you — for example missing profile pictures, empty or near-empty posting history, and other low-quality patterns. Higher scores mean higher ghost/bot risk; lower scores look more like normal accounts.

Score range Typical meaning
0–30 Looks healthy — usual profile signals
31–60 Mixed signals — review before mass actions
61–100 High ghost/bot risk — strong cleanup candidate

Scores are heuristic, not a legal verdict. Always whitelist friends and brands before bulk unfollow.

Ghost Score vs basic unfollowers scripts

Classic console bookmarklets (including many “Instagram Unfollowers” clones) only answer: “Do they follow me back?” Ghost Score answers: “Even if they do — or even before you unfollow — is this account likely fake or dead?” That is the main PRO differentiator for community health.

How to use it safely

  1. Open Instagram in your browser (already logged in — no password to our tool).
  2. Run Instagram Unfollowers PRO via extension, console loader, or mobile bookmarklet.
  3. Scan your following list and sort/filter by Ghost Score.
  4. Whitelist people you care about, then unfollow in small batches with Safe Mode delays.

Everything runs 100% locally. We never ask for your Instagram password and do not upload your follower lists to our servers.
