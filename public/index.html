Frequently Asked Questions

Is Instagram Unfollowers PRO safe to use?

Yes. The tool never asks for your Instagram password and never sends your data to any external server. It runs 100% locally in your browser (or as a browser extension using Shadow DOM) and only talks directly to Instagram using your own already-logged-in session, exactly like the Instagram website itself does.

Do I need to log in or give my password anywhere?

No. You just need to be logged into Instagram normally in your browser. The script reads your existing session cookie to make the same requests your browser already makes — it never collects or stores your credentials.

How is this different from other Instagram unfollowers trackers?

Unlike most console-script clones, Instagram Unfollowers PRO adds a real Ghost Score (0-100) to flag bots and inactive accounts, real-time unfollower alerts, multi-account support, a full history/time-machine view, and professional PDF/CSV exports — all while remaining free and open source.

Can Instagram ban my account for using this?

Any automation carries some risk, which is why the tool includes randomized delays, an automatic "Safe Mode" and an emergency stop that pauses everything if Instagram returns a rate-limit error (429). Using the default timings and unfollowing in small batches keeps the risk very low.

Does it work on mobile (iOS/Android)?

Instagram blocks external script loaders (CSP), so tiny bookmarklets no longer work. Use the browser extension on desktop (recommended), or paste the full Copy Script into the desktop DevTools console on instagram.com.

What is the Growth Engine (Beta)?

Growth (beta) finds users who comment on accounts in your niche and follows them with anti-ban delays and ghost filtering. It is a PRO feature, currently unlocked for free in v8.5.

Is it really free?

Yes. Unlimited scans, ghost detection, PDF health reports and real-time monitoring are all currently free while we're in our open promotional phase. You can also support development voluntarily via Ko-fi.
