How to Detect Instagram Bots in 2026 (Without Risky Password Apps)

Fake followers still flood Instagram. Here is a practical checklist to spot bots — and a safer way to scan your list using a local browser tool instead of handing over your login.

Common signs of bot / spam accounts

  • No profile photo or a generic stock image reused across many accounts
  • Zero or near-zero posts while following thousands of people
  • Username patterns: random letters, many digits, or “growth” spam keywords
  • Bios full of unrelated links, crypto spam, or empty placeholders
  • They follow you (or you follow them) but never like, comment, or story-view

Alone, none of these prove a bot. Together they raise risk — which is what a Ghost Score aggregates into one number.

What not to do

Avoid websites and apps that ask for your Instagram password or “login with Instagram” on an unknown third-party domain. Those can steal sessions. Prefer tools that run inside your already-logged-in browser tab.

A safer workflow in 2026

  1. Stay logged into Instagram.com in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
  2. Use Instagram Unfollowers PRO — extension, console loader (~300 bytes), or mobile bookmarklet. No password field.
  3. Scan who doesn’t follow you back, then filter by Ghost Score / ghost signals.
  4. Whitelist real friends. Unfollow suspects slowly with Safe Mode delays.
  5. Export a PDF/CSV report if you manage brands or multiple accounts.

Can Instagram ban me for cleaning bots?

Instagram rate-limits aggressive automation. Mass unfollow in minutes is riskier than human-paced batches. Use default delays, stop if you see “try again later” / 429 errors, and never run kamikaze speeds on a main account.
